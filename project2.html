Challenges & Solutions

🌍 Challenge: Carbon Footprint Calculation

Problem: Accurately calculating and displaying environmental impact for each product.

Solution: Developed a comprehensive carbon tracking system that factors in materials, manufacturing, shipping, and packaging. Integrated with third-party sustainability APIs to verify data accuracy and provide transparent metrics to customers.

📦 Challenge: Supply Chain Transparency

Problem: Customers wanted complete visibility into product sourcing and ethical practices.

Solution: Built a blockchain-inspired traceability system that tracks each product from raw materials to delivery. Created interactive visualizations showing manufacturing locations, certifications, and sustainability scores.

� Challenge: Inventory Optimization

Problem: Balancing stock levels while minimizing waste and overproduction.

Solution: Implemented AI-powered demand forecasting using historical data and trend analysis. Created smart reorder system that reduces excess inventory by 40% while maintaining 98% product availability.
